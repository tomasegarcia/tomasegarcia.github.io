"The people who need to be heard most are often the hardest to reach — and I want to be the person who can bridge that gap."

My name is Tomás García. I graduated from the University of St. Thomas with degrees in Psychology, Catholic Studies, and Philosophy — three disciplines that are not accidental. Psychology gives me insight into the human person and why we act the way we do. Catholic Studies grounds me in the dignity and worth of every individual. Philosophy equips me with the tools of reasoning in pursuit of truth and justice.

This summer I will be studying international law at the Hague Academy of International Law at the Peace Palace in the Netherlands — the next step toward law school and a career dedicated to advancing justice across cultures and communities.

My father is from Mexico and my mother from a small town in Minnesota. Growing up between two cultures, two languages, and two ways of life gave me the ability to see what exists in the space between them — gaps that are real, but often invisible to those who have only ever lived on one side. My languages expand that reach.
