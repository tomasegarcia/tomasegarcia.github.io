My name is Tomás García. I graduated from the University of St. Thomas with degrees in Psychology, Catholic Studies, and Philosophy — three disciplines that are not accidental. Psychology gives me insight into the human person and why we act the way we do. Catholic Studies grounds me in the dignity and worth of every individual. Philosophy equips me with the tools of reasoning in pursuit of truth and justice.

This summer I will be studying international law at the Hague Academy of International Law at the Peace Palace in the Netherlands — the next step toward law school and a career dedicated to advancing justice across cultures and communities.

My father is from Mexico and my mother from a small town in Minnesota. Growing up between two cultures, two languages, and two ways of life gave me the ability to see what exists in the space between them — gaps that are real, but often invisible to those who have only ever lived on one side. My languages expand that reach.

Research & Coursework

Undergraduate Research · Presented at MUPC 2026, Augsburg University

The Role of Emotional Intelligence in Daily Spiritual Experience

University of St. Thomas

"But their faith is the same. Everyone sits in the same pew. The Mass is the same. Yet, it is obvious that there is a difference in the perceived experience of God. Why do some experience God more than others? I wanted to understand this gap from an empirical, psychological perspective, which led me to conduct this study."

— Tomás García

An independent empirical study conducted at the University of St. Thomas examining whether emotional intelligence predicts daily spiritual experience. Using the Trait Meta-Mood Scale and the Daily Spiritual Experience Scale across 51 participants, the study found that all three EI subscales significantly predicted spiritual experience, with Attention to Feelings emerging as the strongest predictor (β = .363, p = .006), explaining 28% of the variance in the outcome. Presented at the Midwestern Undergraduate Psychology Conference (MUPC) 2026 at Augsburg University.
